# Product Guidelines

## Visual Identity
- **Primary Colors:** Deep Aquatic Blue (#003366), Teal (#008080), and Slate Grey (#708090) to evoke a sense of water, depth, and reliability.
- **Accent Colors:** Sand (#C2B280) and Coral (#FF7F50) for highlights and call-to-action buttons.
- **Typography:** Serif headings (e.g., Playfair Display) for a prestigious, legacy feel, paired with clean Sans-Serif body text (e.g., Roboto or Open Sans) for modern readability.
- **Imagery Style:** High-resolution, professional photography and videography of installations. Use natural lighting to showcase the clarity of water and vibrant colors of fish and plants.

## Tone and Voice
- **Professional & Expert:** Demonstrating deep technical knowledge in aquarium manufacturing and aquatic life care.
- **Trustworthy & Reliable:** Emphasizing the durability of products and the long-standing history of the business.
- **Passionate & Inspiring:** Sharing the wonder of underwater worlds and encouraging users to bring that beauty into their own spaces.

## Content Strategy
- **Storytelling:** Integrate the 1962 legacy into service descriptions to build trust.
- **Visual-First:** Prioritize galleries and videos over heavy text blocks. Every service page should feature at least one major project showcase.
- **Clear Call-to-Action:** Ensure "Contact Us" or "Inquire via WhatsApp" is always accessible but not intrusive.

## User Experience (UX)
- **Mobile-First Design:** Ensure the extensive media gallery is optimized for fast loading and smooth swiping on mobile devices.
- **Intuitive Navigation:** Simple, flat menu structure focusing on Core Services, Gallery, and Legacy.
- **Performance:** Optimize video content to ensure the site remains fast and responsive.
