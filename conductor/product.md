# Initial Concept
Nalanda Aquarium is a legacy business since 1962, specializing in crafting long-lasting, durable aquariums, fish ponds, waterfalls, and terrariums. The project aims to showcase these services and their rich history through a modern web presence.

# Product Vision
To be the premier digital destination for high-end custom aquatic installations in India, bridging a 60-year legacy with modern design and service excellence.

# Target Audience
- **Residential Customers:** Homeowners looking to enhance their living spaces with custom aquariums or koi ponds.
- **Commercial Clients:** Offices, hotels, and public spaces requiring impressive, large-scale aquatic displays.
- **Aquarium Enthusiasts:** Hobbyists seeking expert advice, high-quality livestock, and professional maintenance services.

# Core Goals
- **Service Showcase:** Clearly present the range of services including manufacturing, maintenance, ponds, waterfalls, and terrariums.
- **Visual Portfolio:** Use a high-quality gallery of images and videos to demonstrate craftsmanship and past successes.
- **Legacy & Trust:** Highlight the company's history since 1962 to build credibility and trust with potential clients.

# Key Features
- **Legacy Section:** Detailed history of the brand and its founders.
- **Service Grid:** Clear categorization of offerings.
- **Interactive Gallery:** Support for high-quality images and videos of custom projects.
- **Contact & Inquiry:** Easy-to-find contact information and floating WhatsApp integration for immediate leads.
- **Mobile Responsive Design:** Ensuring a seamless experience across all devices.
