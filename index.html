Our Legacy & History

Nalanda Aquarium carries a rich heritage built over decades of craftsmanship, passion, and dedication to fish keeping.

Founder: M. R. T. Rayappa

Nalanda Aquarium was established in 1962 by M. R. T. Rayappa, a pioneer who introduced quality aquarium craftsmanship and fish-keeping culture to Bengaluru. His vision and commitment laid the foundation for our long-standing reputation.

Master Fish Breeder: P. Kannan

P. Kannan, a renowned fish breeder, has been an integral building member of Nalanda Aquarium. His deep expertise in fish breeding, care, and maintenance continues to help shape our identity and service quality.

Part of Bengaluru’s Aquarium Heritage

We are proud to be part of Bengaluru’s long-standing aquarium heritage, inspired by esteemed pioneers like Wet Pet's Aquarium, Bharath Aquarium, International Aquarium, Sea Bird Aquarium, and Seenu Aquarium.
